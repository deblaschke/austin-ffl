Week 16
(Playoff Semifinals)
NFL Schedule
The Coons 31 Thundering Curd 26
Slushy Lushes* 42 Trouser Trout 20
by Mark Gehring

The Curd falls prey to the Coons in a short lived playoff experience. If the Curd would've played their returning star Rice they would've been in the finals this week. Woulda, shoulda, coulda, pick a seat on the couch with the rest of us. The Coons make their run at being the first team to ever repeat as champion, but come in as underdogs to the powerful Lushes. Let's see if they have one more mile in the tank and can compete with the Lushes... Hey! Curd, stop hogging the salsa and pass the chips...

The Lushes host the Trout to a fish fry and ends the floundering fishes season. What was touted as a Monday night battle turned out to be a 17-0 filleting of the hapless pisces. The Trout can bring a six-pack to the couch and cheer for their West rivals in the ATM Bowl. The Lushes continued to pile up the points, matching their league average and setting their sights on the Coons. One problem for the Lushes, they are bruised and battered and many of their starters may not play this week. Half their team doesn't have to play and others need to throw their game for draft pick placement in the draft. Can the Lushes win with Davis and Elam... will have to wait and see...

Scandal Rips League Office

Scandal ripped the FFL league office this week when an unnamed source reported that the Curd has been playing a receiver they didn't own (Yancy Thigpen). The Curd tried to pick up Thigpen early in the season while the Lushes owned him, they were denied this draft. But, due to a so called paperwork error, the Curd had Thigpen erroneously added to their lineup.

When league commish Ridgeway was asked for a interview he stormed out off the press room and screamed "No comment!.. but it you persist you won't have me to kick around anymore...". The league office is trying to determine if the Curd use payola to pull off this charade or if it was a sex for trade scandal.... The controversy will surely continue to the annual league gathering, if the commish doesn't crack first......

The Commish's comment "I've never even seen the Curd owner naked... Well, there was that one time in the Virgin Islands.." It was a paperwork error. That's all. No money changed hands, I've always owned that Ferrari.

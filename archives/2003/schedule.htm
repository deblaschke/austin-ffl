The Thundering Curd apply two coats of cheese spread to the gridiron and slide past Jablome 26-25. The murmuring milk get a meager 30 and 27 yards out of their receivers, but Ahman is da'man with two rushing touchdowns and 115 combined yards to save the day. "Ja blow, oh, oh me is ta know, oh, oh me" get absolutely nothing out of their passing game emphasized by a Plummer stuck in the toilet, and only a 12-point performance from the league's top pick and a freak-of-nature Detroit defensive touchdown keeps the game as close as it is. The Curd win their first game despite drafting two players who won't even suit up for at least the first month - although both still scored as much as most of the active players on the team - while Jablome, for the third time in four years, change the team name but appear to stink much the same as the owner leaves three double-digit outputs sitting on the bench.

The Horny Toads substitute placebos for Viagra and speed by the lifeless Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love 32-3. The anxious amphibians have their big Macs - top two picks McAllister and McNabb - come up empty in everything but calories, but Chris comes out of his Chambers for 118 receiving yards and two touchdowns and that proves to be way more than is needed. The flaccid foreskins set a new standard in limpidity when their Colt 45s - top two picks Manning and Harrison - show up unloaded and one of the league's top kickers gets shutout Monday night, so only 111 Davis rushing yards light up the scoreboard. The Toads start off undefeated but need to improve their running game if they wish to stay that way while the Purple Helmets waste no time in establishing 2003's record for fewest points in a game and can rest easy knowing it wasn't due to poor lineup decisions because all 16 team members combine for 15 points.

Got Sheep? behave more like bucking broncos as they boot the Ball Busters out of the corral 46-33. The magnificent mutton get points out of every positional player, with the best shows put on by "Daunte's Inferno" (three passing touchdowns) and "Oh, Henry!" (two rushing touchdowns). The cahones crackers do fine aerially, registering the season's first points Thursday evening and adding in 21 more points Sunday afternoon, but the ground attack gets hung up as their top two picks go for naught. The Sheep pick up right up where they left off last year, winning (sixth straight including playoffs) with dorky names like Curtis, Daunte, Corey, Warrick, Travis, Chad, Dorsey, Muhsin and Kordell while the Busters pick up right where they left off last year, losing (sixth straight) with their top two studs playing like duds.

Beer 'n Broads outgas the Itchy Tortugas 27-21 in Flatulence Bowl I - Beer-Induced Versus Burrito-Induced. The promising porters aren't spectacular as their first pick rushes for one touchdown and their second pick catches one touchdown and 150 yards, and it's only their kicker's Monday night nickel that turns a 22-21 nailbiter into the "comfy" six-point victory. The terrible tacos' first two picks combine for a measly three points and a man named Brady throws like a woman for 123 passing yards and no scores, so only Pittsburgh's Hines ketchup man's two receiving touchdowns provide any semblance of an offense. The Broads manage the win but look to be pretty thin - quite contradictory for a team chock full o' beer bellies - as 10 of 16 roster spots produce zero points while the Torts manage to do that one better (11/16 blanks) and suffer at the mistake of starting their number two quarterback over their number one and his 18 points.

The Penguin Rookery bid a fine how-dee-do to Da'renegades with a 54-23 plastering. The tremendous tuxedos sport a trio of flashy performances - 15 points out of number one pick Alexander, 12 points out of number two pick Geezer Gannon, and 12 surprising points out of rookie Rogers. The lost loners get 120 rushing yards and two touchdowns out of Clinton, Denver's next president, but the rest of the team can only manage two sets of performance points and two PATs. The Rooks become the first expansion team in recorded history to win their first game in such an impressive fashion but still have that Hambrick third-round brick hanging around their neck while the Renegades lose at quarterback roulette, opting for their eighth-rounder (zero points) over their second (9 points) and thirteenth (18 points) rounders.

The Thundering Curd back up the dairy truck without checking the rear-view mirror, squishing the Horny Toads 37-17 in the process. The babbling Brie enjoy 160 rushing yards and one touchdown from Green Bay's Green and fish two more touchdowns out of a New Orleans Brooks, then throw in a defensive touchdown for good measure. The frigid froggies record nada from their passing game as only Deuce's one touchdown and Sebastard's 11 kicking points show up on the scoreboard. The Thunderings are undefeated after Week 1 for the first time since Ben Franklin was torching keys on kites while the Hornies look to be at the bottom of the pond's food chain as the entire roster tallies one passing, one rushing and one receiving touchdown.

Got Sheep? pull a Monday night miracle out of the feedbag to escape Jablome 52-50 in a rematch of last year's ATM Bowl participants. The lucky lambs get a miraculous three rushing touchdowns out of Travis in only 26 yards, two touchdowns from Daunte and one from Corey, but it's the freakish defensive touchdown turned in by the Cowpukes Monday night that pushes them over the top after trailing 50-46 going in. The Jablownaway change four of seven starters and double last week's output, but it proves to be futile as it's the two positional players they didn't change that account for a Priestly three touchdowns and 122 rushing yards as well as a Mouldy one touchdown and 133 receiving yards. Got Sheep? defeat their second Commissioner in as many weeks but don't know how much of these close call coronaries the flock can withstand while Jablome might want to consider adding a few more Priests to the roster for spiritual support as they have now lost two games by a combined three points.

The Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love briefly regain some firmness and poke past the Penguin Rookery 25-13. The mauve manpoles finally get a Manning-Marvin hookup good for 12 points, but the only other "offensive" star is the defense as it takes an interception to the house. The nuked nestlings know they are in trouble when one of their starting receivers is guilty of tardiness and sentenced to 60 minutes on the bench and old man Gannon throws for a paltry 103 yards, so Yawn's one touchdown and 51 rushing yards is the only feat worthy of mention. The Love Soldiers improve their point generation by over 800% but are still in a world of hurt as they have yet to cash in a single rushing touchdown by any member of the team this year while the Penguins come crashing back to Earth with the week's low point total after registering the high point total just last week.

The Itchy Tortugas shovel beans into the boiler and steamroll Da'renegades 49-21. The chafing chimichangas get three single touchdown performances, but it is Jamal Lewis who steals the week's headlines as he runs through gaping holes the size of Julia Roberts' mouth in the Cleveland defense to record two touchdowns and an NFL single-game record 295 rushing yards. The raunchy rebels can only brag about getting one passing and one receiving touchdown, and know their afternoon is over when their number one pick takes the second half off after amassing 129 first-half rushing yards. The Itchies break into the win column but better not depend on NFL-record breakers to continue doing so while the Renegades look to be the league punching bag as they suffer the biggest margin of defeat two weeks running and are being outscored at almost a 3-to-1 pace, both of which lead to sole possession of last place in the West.

The Ball Busters blow the head off Beer 'n Broads 56-42. The nut knockers have everyone but their top pick put on a good show, with Hasselbeck (three touchdowns), Coles (one touchdown and 180 receiving yards) and James (one touchdown and 120 rushing yards) assuming the leading roles. The flat Fosters 'n floozies do well with half their starters, but their doom is carved in stone when Randy Mouth and Fraud Taylor pitch shutouts and the older Gramatica has no reason to jump around the field like a baboon after having two field goals and one PAT blocked. The Balls snap their six-game losing streak in style with the week's high output but seem to be a weird blend with the second-best offense and second-worse defense, while the Beer fall flat but still enjoy a four-way tie for first in the West despite their misgivings.

The Itchy Tortugas turn the Thundering Curd into chile con queso with a 32-16 melting. The charging chalupas only get half of last week's production out of ravin' Jamal, but that's still good for nine points and is more than enough - when combined with their kicker's 11 - to earn the double-U. The chundering turd revert to their old (and you know the owner, so we're talking way old here) ways by not turning in a lineup, and the result is not pretty as the receivers combine for 56 yards and the quarterback is the only lost soul to find the end zone - once. The Torts rebound nicely from their opening day loss to record two straight wins but face a few weeks of lawlessness without a Marshall in town while the Curd are exiled from the kingdom of undefeatedland but can't feel too bad about not submitting a lineup since they only left five points riding the pine.

The Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love reverse roles and blow Jablome away 27-6. The underwear rockets do diddly on the ground as one might expect from backs named Ladell and Curtis, but two touchdowns in the air over Peyton's place and yet another defensive touchdown pave the way to Win City. Jablome is dabomb - stink bomb, that is - as the mentally challenged owner benches his top two picks and their united three touchdowns and one 100+ rushing yards performance, and all that's left is a kicker's six and a bunch of losers. The Purple Love is now 2-1 despite having the league's worst offense thanks to the league's best defense - although scores when they are playing remind one of women's soccer - while Jablome maintain a winning percentage full of nothing but zeros and look to keep it that way for a long time to come with coaching decisions like these.

The Horny Toads duel Got Sheep? to a 16-16 tie in the inaugural (and hopefully final) The Only Animals That Belong On A Football Field Are Mascots Bowl I. The French fried frogs can only muster two touchdowns and trail 16-12 after Sunday's action, then have to wait with baited breath while their Polish prick of a kicker skips bail after another bar brawl and pooches four points through the uprights Monday night. The flock-ewes put on a clinic - literally - of sandlot football as one starter shows up late and three leave early with injuries, but fortunately Daunte manages to stumble across the goal line twice for a much-needed dozen. The Toads must be into mirrors as both their record (1-1-1) and points for/against (65/56) are perfect reflections while the Sheep appear to need more rehab than Mariah Carey and Robert Downey Jr. combined and better start scouring the high schools (now now, don't go there, the owner is married) for enough healthy bodies to field seven next week.

Beer 'n Broads prove that alcohol and young'uns don't mix as they waste the Penguin Rookery 36-10. Bass 'n ass make a last-minute lineup change and it pays off in spades as rookie Texan Andre snags two touchdowns along with 102 receiving yards, then watch in enjoyment as first picky Ricky bulldozes 42 carries into ten more points. The flailing flipperwings pull the ultimate newbie error by not paying attention to bye weeks and start a Cowboy enjoying his weekend in a saloon (although in a strict mathematical sense, Hambrick on bye = Hambrick playing), so the rest of the team assumes it's just a bye week for everyone and heads off to join him early. B'nB are the high scorers out West - quite unusual since the owner hardly ever scores - while the Penguins seem to have cooled down in the frigid Arctic waters, averaging only 15 points per game over the last two weeks.

Da'renegades use chocolate-covered peanut clusters to bat the Ball Busters 23-13. The drab deserters have zero ground game, but broken Kelly hangs around long enough to hurl two touchdown passes and sir Isaac catches his first six-pointer of the year to save the day. The odoriferous orbs get a lot of nothing out of a lot of big names such as Torry Dolt, Sue Edge James, a stooge named Moe, and a bricklayer named Mason, and even all-world Brett suffers a major meltdown in the hot Arizona sun. The renegades finally taste the sweetness that is the victory column although they only draw one other bye week versus the Balls this year while the Busters go against the prime fantasy football directive - never sit your studs - by benching their top QB, RB and WR in favor of "better" matchups, and the lost 18 points cost them the game.

The Thunders have a .750 winning percentage for the first time since Tonya Harding attended charm school while the Renegades now field the most pathetic offense in the league and have only a female owner's ignorance of the prime directive to thank for their lone win.

The Ball Busters take the air out of Jablome 51-28. The battling bulbs fall back on their studs and it pays off as LT2 racks up two touchdowns - including a ultrarare one passing to a quarterback - and 187 rushing yards and Torry nabs one touchdown and 133 receiving yards, so Brett's Monday night stats (18 points) aren't even needed. The Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love explode in Mt. Etna-like fashion all over the Itchy Tortugas 94-38 in Spooge Fest '03. The amazing appendages spew forth in every way possible when all positional players nail paydirt - the Mann raises the bar passing with SIX touchdowns and 314 yards, half of those (3/158 respectively) aimed at his Marvin-elous receiver, Davis continues his streak of 100+ yard rushing games and finally adds a touchdown, and Ashley and Ladell each add a touchdown despite the fact they sound like they should be singing some gay '80's love song as a duet. The shot tamales dust off the shelves and start two Ram backups, and their four combined touchdowns along with another good Jamal day (nine points) make a decent showing, but nowhere near enough in this Peyton pass whumping. The Helmeted Soldiers enter the Hall of Fame by one point for most points in a game after almost doubling their offensive output from the first three games while the Itchies fall back to .500 after suffering as thorough a beating as was seen in Peewee Herman's adult cinema incident when one line tells it all - Manning 39, Torts 38.

Beer 'n Broads give the Horny Toads a severe case of limp stick 53-25. Harp 'n tramps pull a drunken newbie-like maneuver when the owner turns in a lineup that starts Stacey Mack twice, but fortunately Randy Mossome has his way with the gay San Francisco defense to the tune of three touchdowns and 172 receiving yards, and Vanderjagt (13) and Bledsoe (12) reach double digits as well. The craving croakers do nada via air when their newly acquired Spurrier disciple joins both receivers in scoring zero, but both backs register a touchdown and Drunkinkowski boots a dime to keep the contest from getting really embarrassing. The Beers move to 3-1 without their number one pick but may not continue being so lucky if they keep making lineup blunders while the Hornies slip under after two consecutive weeks at .500 thanks to getting only 3 points out of six receivers.

The Penguin Rookery flipper-slap Got Sheep? 39-37 in yet another close animal contest. The stellar ice-dwellers pull a Jiminy Cricket when the owner wishes for a Hambrick breakout and actually gets it (a touchdown and 127 rushing yards), but it's old man Gannon with his three touchdowns and 348 passing yards that make the difference. The reeling rams still suffer health issues as they start one broken running back and another whose crotch gives out in the second quarter (just like last week), but Flair McNair's three touchdowns and Chad's two keep the game closer than it should. The Rookery squeak out a close one but need to pay more attention to breaking news since they start David Bust-on, who was suspended Thursday afternoon for being himself, and have now not started a full lineup for three weeks in a row while the Gots are winless for two weeks running and might want to try a revolutionary concept such as picking up working free agents to replace their non-working regulars, although that's probably not in the family budget.

The Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love penetrate Got Sheep?'s backfield 57-40. The powerful prongs get the usual 150+ rushing yards and one touchdown out of a Panther named Stephen but that's about it as they trail 40-25 going into Monday night, but two Manning/Marvin hookups and a ton of yardage push them over the hump. The little blow peeps start a kicker on I-R since Week 1 and still have a bum quarterback, but the much older version fills in Titanically with 391 passing yards and two touchdowns and the team hoards deuces - two defensive safeties plus a two-point conversion passing - but it's far from enough to overcome their Monday night meltdown. The Purple Love seem to righted the ship as they now possess the league's most potent offense despite hanging a three-spot Week 1 while the Sheep need to focus because they haven't fielded a complete team for two weeks now, although it wouldn't have made a difference as Monday night in Tampa must have felt a lot like Saturday in Ames.

The Horny Toads leapfrog Da'renegades 36-23. The second plague of Egypt descend upon their opponents with assistance from a Ram (sey) with a rushing and passing touchdown, a Seabass with five field goals, and an Eagle with a defensive touchdown. The plunging peons think they're in good shape as they anticipate the opening of Fiedler on the Roof, but the Ivy leaguer with the peewee arm throws for nada, and only Clinton's nine and Olindo's 12 prove to be of any value. The Toads snap a three-game winless streak and get back to .500 despite scoring less on the year than the 0-5 East cellar-dwellers while the Renegades continue to suck up a storm and look to have as much chance of making the playoffs as Rush Limbaugh has of being the Grand Master of Ceremonies at a parade in Philadelphia.

In the second stinkfest of this young season, Beer 'n Broads again outgas the Itchy Tortugas, this time with a much more pungent 44-14 victory in Flatulence Bowl II. Hooray Beer! get another vocal performance out of Randy Mouth, who registers his fourth and fifth touchdowns in two weeks, then watch in amazement as fragile Freddy puts up 164 yards of total offense with both a rushing and receiving touchdown. The terrible tostadas have their starting quarterback and both running backs out on byes, but the owner's choice of replacements accomplish the same score as the missing members when Mad Ox Tommy is pulled after 136 pitiful yards and both Philly backs combine for a near worthless 39 rushing yards. The Broads begin to pull away in the West, unlike the owner who could never pull away from a buffet table, while the Torts are in a world of hurt if the scoreboard can't distinguish between an inactive Itchy and an active Itchy (sounds like an ad on VD).

The Penguin Rookery outnudge the Ball Busters 23-11 in the first (and hopefully last) A Touchdown, A Touchdown, My Kingdom For A Touchdown Bowl. The formally attired flightless fowl are blessed with several good matchups but screw up all but the one where Shaun the Seahawk runs for 102 yards and one touchdown, with the kicker doing the rest. For the second straight week the testes ticklers waste $2 on a bucking Fronco and then do the Rookery one better by not having anyone find the end zone, with the kicker doing all the work - heck, one player can't even find a stadium shaped like a sombrero Monday night. The Penguins get back over .500 but may not be able to stay there long since they don't get to play the Busters ten more times this year while the Balls are behaving like yo-yos, much like their owner, as they continue to bounce up and down in both scores and wins/losses over the last four weeks - 56 (W), 13 (L), 51 (W) and 11 (L) - and much like the Edge this past week, who's gonna play, then not gonna play, then gonna play, then not gonna play.

And now a few awards to close out the season, all chosen unanimously by a 1-0 vote from the league's panel of expert:

Most Valuable Player: Priest Holmes, Jablome
This will probably spark one of those great player on a lousy team debates, but for the second straight year Priest looked like a man playing amongst boys, much like Michael Jackson at Neverland Ranch. Yes, Jamal Lewis put up the second-most rushing yards (2066) in NFL history to go along with 14 touchdowns. And yes, Ahman Green put up the most touchdowns (20) in Packer history to go along with 1883 rushing yards. But it's fantasy football, folks, and points is points. Priest scored 27 rushing touchdowns, which broke the NFL single-season records for most rushing touchdowns (topping Emmitt's 25 in '95) and most total touchdowns (surpassing Marshall's 26 in '00). Throw in 2110 total yards of offense and Holmes was a Sherlock to score week in and week out, averaging 125 yards and 1½ touchdowns per week. How an owner can turn such production into the league's worst record is unfathomable, especially if you consider that the team also fielded a second all-AFFL'er in Jeff Wilkins, who tied an NFL record for most field goals in a single season, and had six players score in triple digits for the year, which tied the champion Ball Busters for best in the league. Repeat, unfathomable. Perhaps Priest's teammates should just say "Jablome, coach, Jablome."

Coach of the Year: Blake Zipoy, Beer 'n Broads
Two words - thirteen, two. The best regular season record in recorded league history, thanks to the tragic Windoze crash of 1996 that caused all AFFL records from prior years to be lost at C. And this feat was accomplished with five sticks in the top six picks - Ricky Williams in round one (10 TDs, which ain't bad except for the second overall pick), Fred Taylor in round three (seven TDs), Amani Toomer in round four (five TDs), Rod Smith in round five (three TDs) and Blew Dredsoe in round six (13 TDs). Still nothing overly spectacular, yet this roster managed to produce four 1000+ yard receivers (with two more hitting the 900's), three 1000+ yard rushers and a scoring differential of 225 points (again, the best in recorded league history) thanks to the year's most potent offense (638 points) and the most restrictive defense (413 points). Sometimes you can find Armani at Wal-Mart.

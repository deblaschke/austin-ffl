2003 Draft Party
(August 28, 2003 at Hooters in Round Rock)

The owner of the Horny Toads, right, is more than happy to offer good advice to the newbie owner of the Penguin Rookery, such as how drafting MVP-candidate Troy Hambrick with the 21st overall pick is quite the steal
The league's status monkey presents a rare view of the typical wacked-out cheatsheet used by the owner of the Itchy Tortugas to pull off his annual drafting magic
The owner of the Thundering Curd, center, is overwhelmingly happy at being able to draft every Packer who can possibly score this year except Brett Favre
The owner of Beer 'n Broads, left, can't hide his "Oops, can you smell it?" expression while the owner of the Penguin Rookery tries desperately to pretend he didn't hear or smell anything
The owner of Got Sheep?, left, sports his favorite ISU shirt and grabs for a Natty Light in tribute to his fallen idol, Larry Eustachy (the league's status monkey has no idea who let


Gandhi

into the party for a second straight year, but credits the spiritual leader with saving money by reusing 2002's cheatsheet)
The owner of the Ball Busters, right, is overly amused at the robot impression performed by the owner of the Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love
The owners of the Itchy Tortugas, left, and Da'renegades bow their head in homage to the picture of Arny Palmer and his four-hootered army
The owner of Da'renegades can't hide his joy as he blurts out "I'll take one Jay Fiedler to go, please!"
The evening's lovely waitperson collects the tabs, but the owner of the Horny Toads, right, doesn't appear to be interested in her money
In continuing a time-honored tradition at these events, the league's status monkey snaps an out-of-focus picture - this time of a beer glass - yet is still very happy with the final result...
...So happy in fact that he snaps a close-up too because, let's face it, that's a really nice glass

The owner of the Ball Busters shows up way early because she's so anxious to see her league buddies (in other words, collect her winnings)

The owners of the Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love, right, and Jablome revel in delight at their continued dominance as league Kisses of Death
The owner of Da'renegades, right, gets a crazy look in his eye after the owner of the Thundering Curd happily informs him that he's going to draft five Packers and Jay Fiedler in the first six rounds next year
Time seems to stand still as the owner of the Itchy Tortugas is accosted by the evening's lovely waitperson

The owners of the Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love, right, and Jablome talk strategy as they discuss which players they're going to sentence to the I-R next year
The owner of Da'renegades tries desperately to pretend there's no activity worth noticing over his left shoulder

Yet another outing drinking only ice-cold non-alcoholic beverages seems to have paid off again for the owner of Da'renegades as the lovely waitperson simply known as "Red" affords him his annual feel

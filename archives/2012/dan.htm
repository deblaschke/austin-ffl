DAN MARINO - Former Miami Dolphins quarterback Dan Marino was introduced as spokesman for the American Association of Retired Persons and is officially their "Men's Life Ambassador", meaning he will give his knowledge on everything from men's health, fitness, sports, lifestyle, aging and more, to members of the organization. Marino, currently an NFL studio analyst for CBS, said he plans to "create awareness and when you turn 50 there's new challenges and things you can do. It's not the end."

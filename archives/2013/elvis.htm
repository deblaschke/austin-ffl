ELVIS DUMERVIL AND PALS (MARCH 15) - Denver Broncos defensive end Elvis Dumervil was released just before the 4PM EST deadline for restructured contracts because the necessary paperwork for the deal wasn't filed in time - the Broncos gave Dumervil's agent, Marty Magid, a 3PM deadline to accept the deal but the team didn't hear from Dumervil's camp until 3:25PM at which point a verbal agreement was reached, but Magid failed to send the fax through on time and the team was forced to waive Dumervil at 3:59PM.

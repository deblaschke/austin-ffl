AARON HERNANDEZ (JUNE 26) - New England Patriots tight end Aaron Hernandez was charged with first-degree murder in the shooting death of Odin Lloyd, in addition to five gun-related charges; Massachusetts State Police obtained a search warrant after evidence surfaced that Hernandez intentionally destroyed his home security system, a cell phone belonging to Hernandez was turned over to police "in pieces", and Hernandez allegedly hired a "team of house cleaners" the same day Lloyd's body was discovered.

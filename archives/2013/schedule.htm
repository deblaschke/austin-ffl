Sure, kiss-virgin Marullo had a painful Week 5, losing both David Wilson (neck) and Julio Jones (foot) for the year, but that's the sum total of the team's injuries. All that pales in comparison to the one week Fraley had - unlucky Week 7, when three of five offensive starters went to heaven, along with the team's playoff aspirations - Jay Cutler (groin) until Week 14, top-pick Arian Foster (neck) for the year, and Reggie Wayne (knee) for the year. Of the two offensive starters that survived the carnage, one (Sydney Rice) was lost for the year (knee) the following week. Throw in the longest ledger of minor boo-boos, and Ace is king... of winning KOD, breaking a three-way tie for most with his fourth. That'd be the one and only four-play he's good at...

Biggest Waste of Talent Award: Jamaal Charles, Rhoads Roadies
After "racking up" 24 TDs in the 65 games spanning his first five seasons, including peaks of only seven rushing (2009) and three receiving (2010), Charles seemed slightly worthy of his late second round draft position. However, J-Mall busted out in style in 2013, rewarding his owner with 19 TDs - 12 over the ground, seven through the air - on 1980 total yards to wind up as the #1 fantasy running back by far with 134 points, 31 more than LeSean McCoy. Unfortunately, that was also 17 TDs more than the back (C.J. Spiller) chosen by the Roadies ahead of Charles, and the rest of the team didn't fare much better. In fact, Jamaal ended up with 33% more rushing TDs than all other RBs on the roster combined, and even finished with the most receiving TDs too. There's no doubt that Charles was in charge for Rhoads, but his supporting cast played more like the chubbos from The Facts of Life.

Shame On Me Award: Stephanie Blaschke, Ball Busters
Fool me once, shame on you, fool me twice...

In this case, the fooler is New Orleans Saints head coach Sean Payton while the foolee is Ball Busters owner Stephanie Blaschke. The escapade began back in Week 13 of 2009 when the Busters lost to Beer 'n Broads 28-25... because Payton deactivated their only active kicker, John Carney, Sunday morning in favor of Garrett Hartley, depriving them of 15 points and a victory (their other kicker was on a bye, and this was back before free agent transactions were allowed on weekends). Had the Busters won, they would have won the East by a game over Beer 'n Broads and headed off to the playoffs. Fast forward to Week 2 of this year when the Busters lost to the Georgia Bullheads 35-34... because Payton took a Hartley field goal off the board after a defensive penalty gave the Saints first and goal at the 1, and the four subsequent plays resulted in a turnover on downs. Had the Busters won, they would have finished a half-game ahead of the Rac Seekers for the last wildcard spot and headed off to the playoffs. So, to summarize, the Busters were two Payton-screwovers away from seven consecutive playoff appearances - two better than the current record held by the Horny Toads.

Editor's Note: The owner of the Ball Busters has already petitioned the league front office to rename this the FSP Award... not exactly sure what it means, but might have something to do with Sean Payton.

Retrospective Award: Frank Marullo, Itchy Tortugas
Hats off to the highly offensive nature of last year's Itchy Tortugas' squad, as the first, second and fourth picks from this year's draft were on that highest scoring team from 2012... that somehow ended up in last place in their division. How did that happen again? Anyway, such a collection of top picks from one team hasn't been seen since back in 2008, when the first, second and third picks were on the highest scoring team from 2007, the Ball Busters. Of course, that squad actually had a winning record and took home the ATM Bowl trophy.

Giant Leap of Faith Award: Pat Nogay, DaBurgh Bears
Two bits of advice the status monkey offered the newbie owner of DaBurgh Bears were don't put all your eggs in one basket and don't be a homer. Steeee-rike one! Steeee-rike two! Of course, if you didn't follow either of these suggestions and you are a Broncos fan, you did quite well this year - imagine how many points the Itchy Tortugas would have scored with Knowshon Moreno and Demaryius Thomas in the lineup, too. But in the vast majority of cases, the results won't be near as appealing. Like, for instance, lifetime-Giants fan Pat, who hitched his wagon to the second-worst offense in the NFC and ended up with the #20 QB (Eli Manning), #32 and #112 WRs (Victor Cruz and Hakeem Nicks) and #26 PK (Josh Brown)... and 3-11-1 record. See, how it works is, if the quarterback does bad, the rest of the team... well, you know how it goes. Good thing Pat passed on David Wilson (#97 RB ranked in top 10 at draft)... or maybe Frank just got to him first, dang it! The bad news is it's an expensive lesson ($66), the good news is it's the first pick in the second round of next year's draft.

NFL SCOUT (FEBRUARY 27) - The NFL is investigating the questioning of Colorado tight end Nick Kasa at the combine, where he said a team asked, "Do you like girls?" Kasa recounted what happened to ESPN Radio Denver: "They ask you like, 'Do you have a girlfriend?' Are you married?' Do you like girls?'" Kasa said in the interview Tuesday. "Those kinds of things, and you know it was just kind of weird. But they would ask you with a straight face, and it's a pretty weird experience altogether."

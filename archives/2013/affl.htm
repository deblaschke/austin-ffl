The owner of the now-defunct Da'renegades graciously submitted this photo and has plenty of ideas but has only provided the first sample to get things going. Got caption? Participants may submit as many captions as they choose. Names of participants will remain hidden until after judging. The decision of the judges is final. Contest ends September 16, 2013. OCTOBER 3, 2013 - Seven judges with questionable football knowledge, questionable senses of humor and - most importantly - no entries in the contest voted for their three favorite captions. The final results are shown above in green, where three points were given for a first place vote, two for second and one for third (total first place votes in parentheses). With a sampling size of only seven for 26 entries, the results are far from scientific, but that's what you get when you can't even get everyone in the league to help *cough* Frank *cough* Vince. With a mere two points separating the top eight entries, Craig takes home the two 1st and one 2nd place winners while the monkey (2), Boz (2) and Craig all tie for 3rd. Perhaps a better way to look at the voting results is points per entry: monkey 2.5, Craig 2.375, Greg 2.0, Boz 0.667. That 0.667 is awfully low, especially compared to the rest of the competition, but at least Boz got all of the Rex and Jet-hatred out of his system. Maybe not. Uh, probably not.

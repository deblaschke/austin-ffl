Sure enough, Da'renegades were "awarded" the not-so-coveted tenth ping pong ball in the lottery. And almost sure enough, Da'renegades wound up with a 7-6-2 record, or one game over .500. With psychic accuracy like that the monkey has some simple advice for the owner: keep those predictions coming and next time set your sights higher, don't sell yourself short - at least when it comes to height...

Two-out-of-three Four-out-of-five Ain't Bad Award: Frank Marullo, Itchy Tortugas
Ya gotta give props where props are due - over the last lustrum the Itchy Tortugas have strung together quite an impressive run, reaching the ATM Bowl an amazing four times. That's 80% folks, and more visits to the ultimate showdown than any other franchise in league history save one - the Ball Busters - and accomplished in a mere five years During the four "salad" years of that half-decade the Torts amassed a .625 winning percentage (36-21-3) and, once they got to the playoffs, they started off strong, going 4-0 in the first round while outscoring their opponents almost 2-to-1 (184-100). Unfortunately, much like the owner's inability to make it to home plate from third base, the team couldn't go all the way when it mattered most, going 1-3 in the championship tilt while being outscored 149-176. But probably the most impressive part of the whole story is from where the journey began to make the promised land - the Torts' first two picks were the 5th and 15th in 2003, 10th and 18th in 2004, 2nd and 15th in 2006, and 6th and 20th in 2007. That's some really average-to-low picks, and perhaps provides insight into the owner's burning desire to walk the waiver wire - 44 transactions over that span. Laugh all you want, but it seems to work. At least lately. Not so sure about the ten-odd years prior to this run...

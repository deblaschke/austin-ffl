MICHAEL VICK AND CANINE PALS (JULY 17) - A federal grand jury has indicted Atlanta Falcons quarterback Michael Vick on the charge of "conspiracy to commit interstate commerce in aid of unlawful activities and to sponsor a dog in an animal-fighting venture" after he and three associates procured a property in Virginia - which he sold for less than half its assessed value just two weeks after the initial raid - for the purpose of staging dogfights, bought dogs, and then - under the name Bad Newz Kennels - fought them there and in several other states over a six-year period.

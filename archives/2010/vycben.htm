VINCE YOUNG AND CEDRIC BENSON - Vince and Cedric, togther again. It's like peanut butter and jelly. Burgers and fries. Pickles and ice cream. Unicorns and glitter. No? Well, within a two week period the former Texas Longhorn backfield teammates each got arrested... for throwing a punch... in a Texas bar. Cincinnati Bengals running back Cedric Benson was arrested and charged with misdemeanor assault in connection with a May 30 incident in Austin where he punched an employee at a downtown bar. Not to be outdone, Tennessee Titans quarterback Vince Young was issued a Class C assault citation stemming from a June 13 incident at a Dallas-area strip club where a video tape showed Young throwing a punch that started a bigger ensuing fight.

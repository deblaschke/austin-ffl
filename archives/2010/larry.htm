LARRY JOHNSON AND STUDENTS (JULY 17) - Washington Redskins running back Larry Johnson has been able to avoid jail despite completing just nine of his sentenced 40 hours of community service - Johnson was supposed to work with children at the Kansas City Police Athletic League for his 40 hours of court-ordered service, but he refused to play sports with the children and instead told police officials he wanted to start an art program, which didn't draw enough students and quickly fizzled out.

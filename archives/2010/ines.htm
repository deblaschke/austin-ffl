INES SAINZ - Ines Sainz, the self-proclaimed "hottest sports reporter in Mexico" and former pageant queen, has made a name for herself with her sexy sideline attire. Still, she maintains that she was dressed appropriately during a recent Jets' practice that resulted in controversy. According to Sainz, she was greeted with catcalls in the Jets' locker room while attempting to interview Mark Sanchez. She also believes that head coach Rex Ryan called for a drill in which Sanchez would intentionally overthrow teammates so that they could "accidentally" collide with Sainz. "Jeans and a white button-downed blouse with boots is not wearing something inappropriate," Sainz tweeted.

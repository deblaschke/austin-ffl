TROY POLAMALU (AUGUST 30) - The hair of Pittsburgh Steelers safety Troy Polamalu, which he wears long - three feet long - as a tribute to his Samoan heritage, has been insured for $1 million by Head and Shoulders, the shampoo brand that he endorses; the insurance was obtained through Lloyd's of London, which did not reveal what must be done to the hair for anyone to collect on the policy.

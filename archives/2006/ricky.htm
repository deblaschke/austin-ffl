RICKY WILLIAMS (FEBRUARY 20) - According to numerous sources, including KDVR-TV in Denver and the Miami Herald, Miami Dolphins embattled running back Ricky Williams has violated the NFL's substance abuse policy for a fourth time and faces a one year suspension, prompting agent Drew Rosenhaus to concede that if Williams loses his appeal, the ramifications are obvious: "It's very unlikely he would ever play again or anyone would take a chance on him again."

JAKE PLUMMER (MAY 24) - Denver Broncos quarterback Jake Plummer has been summoned by Englewood, Colorado police for his alleged involvement in a road-rage, hit-and-run type incident last month in which Plummer got out of his gray Honda Element, kicked the front of the car behind him, returned to his vehicle, reversed back into the victim's car, and then pushed the vehicle back for about 10 seconds before speeding away from the scene.

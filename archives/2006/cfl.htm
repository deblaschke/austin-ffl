ONTERRIO SMITH AND RICKY WILLIAMS (MAY 28) - Miami Dolphins running back Ricky Williams, suspended by the NFL for the 2006 season thanks to drug policy violations, signed a $240,000, one-year contract with the Canadian Football League's Toronto Argonauts, joining fellow running back and drug policy violator Onterrio Smith of the Minnesota Vikings, who signed a one-year contract with the Winnipeg Blue Bombers earlier this month.

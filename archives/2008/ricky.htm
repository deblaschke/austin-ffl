RICKY WILLIAMS - Miami Dolphins running back Ricky Williams admitted that he was tempted to smoke marijuana during the Dolphins' bye in Week 4, saying "There was definitely an urge. But I just thought about what I have to lose and it was easy. The urge didn't last very long. I've done a lot of work at understanding myself a little bit more. So I recognize the urge was just a result of the feeling of being free, and I was just trying to maximize it. And I realize that I really enjoy meditating and when I can go home and sit in my room and meditate, I can get the same feeling."

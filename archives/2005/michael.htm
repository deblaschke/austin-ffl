MICHAEL VICK AND SONYA ELLIOT (APRIL 5) - Claiming that Atlanta Falcons quarterback Michael Vick gave her herpes, Sonya Elliot, a 26-year-old Georgia health care worker, is suing the NFL star for negligence and battery and, in a related court filing, her lawyers are seeking Vick's admission that he used a "Ron Mexico" alias - and perhaps other fake names - "for the purpose of herpes testing and/or treatment."

2009 Draft Party
(September 3, 2009 at Hooters in Round Rock)

The owner of the Horny Toads dons a shirt advertising his innate ability in Selecting Extremely Rotten Teams Of Mediocre Athletes
The owner of Rhoads Roadies is more interested in Stony Point football updates on the ol' iPhone than the draft - truly a stoner's pointless approach
The league's elder statesmen - the owners of the Itchy Tortugas, left, and 4Hour Wood - take time out of the draft to pose for a picture, although the latter's  mind  eyes seem to be somewhere else ... oh yeah, Hooters!
The evening's lovely waitperson distracts the owner of 4Hour Wood while her two compatriots give the old codger a two on their finger scale... that'd be one finger each
The owner of 4Hour Wood has tiny little protuberances sticking out in all the wrong places when he cops a feel from the evening's lovely waitperson
That napkin around the Bud is the closest the owner of 4Hour Wood has been to a beer bra in a long time
As the night drags on and high school football games end, the owner of Rhoads Roadies turns to downloading fart jokes on his iPhone for entertainment
After years of decent success, the owner of the Ball Busters shakes things up by basing her draft strategy off hints from The Big Picture Book of Fantasy Football - and scribblings from folks who checked it out in previous years - instead of that newfangled internet stuff



2009 Postseason Party
(January 22, 2010 at Twin Peaks in Round Rock)

It took less than 30 minutes after receiving the league plaque before the owner of 4Hour Wood spilled greasy chicken wing droppings all over it, even though he blamed the baby
The owner of 4Hour Wood poses with the evening's lovely waitperson who, from the expression, seems to be wondering if that's a golf tee in his pocket or if he's just happy to see her
The owner of 4Hour Wood vaguely resembles Rodin's The Thinker - minus the fine muscle tone - as he poses with the league plaque... perhaps he's thinking (and rightfully so) that he'll never get his name on such a fine plaque ever again
The owners of 4Hour Wood, Rhoads Roadies and Ball Busters (left to right) strike a pose as the only owners in league history to win three championships... and NOT do something majorly gay like steal the league plaque
Apparently, after going 3½ hours into his latest four-hour episode, the owner of 4Hour Wood, left, will hit on anything, including the owner of Rhoads Roadies - perhaps it's time to check the dosage of that little blue pill with a credible physician instead of ordering "the strongest stuff ya got" over the internet
The owner of the Ball Busters, left, sympathizes with the owner of the Rac Seekers when it comes to losing that first ATM Bowl appearance... of course, at least she played a quarterback - granted, it was Boomer Esiason, but at least she picked up somebody

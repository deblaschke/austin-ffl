BRETT FAVRE - Retired/unretired/retired quarterback Brett Favre unretired yet again and reported to Minnesota Vikings traing camp after signing a two-year deal worth $25M. The following table chronicles the incredulous timeline of Favre's (un)retirement events:
03/04/08 Favre tells Packers he will retire
03/06/08 Favre retires from Packers, gives tearful retirement press conference
03/27/08 Favre tells Packers head coach Mike McCarthy and general manager Ted Thompson he made mistake by retiring
06/20/08 Favre calls McCarthy with plans to unretire, McCarthy says team has moved on with new starter Aaron Rodgers
07/11/08 Favre asks for unconditional release from Packers to play for another team, Packers deny
07/29/08 Favre faxes paperwork to NFL to be reinstated
07/30/08 Packers president and CEO Mark Murphy meets with Favre in Mississippi to persuade Favre to remain retired, floating $20 million offer over 10 years
08/03/08 NFL reinstates Favre
08/04/08 Favre reports to Packers training camp and meets with McCarthy and Thompson, both sides agree to part ways
08/07/08 Packers trade Favre to Jets for conditional fourth round pick in 2009 draft after refusing to trade him to interested team (Vikings) within division
02/11/09 Favre tells Jets he will retire
04/28/09 Jets waive Favre off their reserved/retired list for unknown reasons, making Favre free to sign with any team should he unretire
05/07/09 Favre tells Vikings head coach Matt Childress he wants to remain retired
07/09/09 Vikings trainer Eric Sugarman visits Mississippi for second time since June to check Favre's physical condition
07/15/09 Favre tells Associated Press that Vikings will know by the July 30 start to their training camp whether he will be attending
07/28/09 Childress tells Star Tribune that Favre will stay retired
08/18/09 Favre signs two-year deal with Vikings

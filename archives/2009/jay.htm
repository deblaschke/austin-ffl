JAY CUTLER (MARCH 31) - Denver Broncos owner Pat Bowlen released a statement saying that the team will try to meet Jay Cutler's trade demands after the disgruntled quarterback learned that the team shopped him to the New England Patriots for Matt Cassel, couldn't work things out in a face-to-face meeting with new coach head Josh McDaniels, and refused to return McDaniels or Bowlen's texts and phone calls after that meeting.

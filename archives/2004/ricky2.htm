RICKY WILLIAMS - When running back Ricky Williams abandoned the Miami Dolphins a few days before training camp, leaving at least $3.6 million on the table and buying a one way ticket to somewhere in Asia, he destroyed the immediate future of a franchise and made false all the previous conclusions that a social disorder was responsible for his history of weirdness. Said Ricky, "I didn't quit football because I failed a drug test, I failed a drug test because I was ready to quit football."

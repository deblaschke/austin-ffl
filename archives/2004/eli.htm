ELI AND ARCHIE MANNING (APRIL 24) - The San Diego Chargers chose Mississippi quarterback Eli Manning to open the NFL draft, despite pleas from the quarterback and his family not to, then traded him to the New York Giants for Philip Rivers after the Mannings, through father Archie and agent Tom Condon, told San Diego general manager A.J. Smith not to take Eli and that Eli was prepared to sit out the season if they did choose him.

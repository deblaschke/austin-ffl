BRANDON MARSHALL (APRIL 22) - Miami Dolphins wide receiver Brandon Marshall was stabbed in the abdomen with a kitchen knife by his wife at the couple's home in Southwest Ranches, Florida; Marshall initially told police he slipped and fell onto a broken glass vase before recanting, similar to what happened back in 2008 when he claimed he slipped on an empty McDonald's bag and put his arm through a television set before admitting he was wrestling around with his family.

LOUIS MURPHY (APRIL 3) - Oakland Raiders wide receiver Louis Murphy was arrested in Gainesville, FL, and charged with possession of a drug without a valid prescription, failure to obey a police officer and resisting arrest without violence; a search of his car turned up an unlabeled prescription bottle containing 11 individual pills later identified as Viagra for which Murphy could not provide a prescription, saying he had peeled the label off of the bottle because he did not want his girlfriend to know he had a prescription for it.

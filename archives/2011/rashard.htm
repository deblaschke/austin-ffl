RASHARD MENDENHALL AND PAL (MAY 2) - Pittsburgh Steelers running back Rashard Mendenhall caused quite a stir with a pair of tweets the day following the announcement of Osama bin Laden's death: "What kind of person celebrates death? It's amazing how people can HATE a man they have never even heard speak. We've only heard one side..." and "We'll never know what really happened. I just have a hard time believing a plane could take a skyscraper down demolition style."

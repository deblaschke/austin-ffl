The Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love suffer through a devastating anti-climax as they battle the Ball Busters to a 37-37 tie. The hurtin' hot dogs think they have the game in the sack when a Patriotic Roman scoops up a fumble late in the fourth quarter Monday night and stumbles into the end zone, only to have the call overturned because replay showed that the Buster's back-up quarterback was clearly down. The nut knockers get a passing touchdown out of one of their receivers, but leave the contest unsatiated as their running backs rack up 307 total yards but only four lousy points. Both teams experience serious déjà vu as they pick up exactly where they left off last year - the Soldiers scoring 37 points in an unvictorious effort while the Busters neither win nor lose again.

Got Sheep? get overstimulated and cream all over the Thundering Curd 48-37. The horny herd have a pretty good weekend as every position player scores except for the Patriotic T. Brown receiver, with a pair of dozens coming from their quarterback and kicker. The miserable mush get 25 points from two of their hometown heroes, but the rest of the team falls to pieces as they watch my favorite Martin limp off the field at halftime. The Sheep seem a little mystified as to why the league's first-overall pick gets outscored by their "other" back while the Thunderings seem elated that their kicker leads the team in scoring, thereby justifying (for the first time in league history) that kickers are worthy of such high draft picks - although that joy must be tempered by the fact that they are the only team in the East with a losing record.

The Itchy Tortugas unleash toxic Mexican-food fumes and sneak past the dazed and confused Enlightened Rogues 31-25 in the inaugural Georgetown Bowl. The brash rash walk away with a victory despite being majorly off-center, as the three components of their passing game put up all but one of the team's points. The not-so-knowledgeable gnomes burst out to a 12-0 Thursday lead, then sit back and watch their failure materialize as nobody manages to reach double digits and two of their top three picks get blanked. That obnoxious sound off in the distance is undoubtedly the Itchies blowing their own horn despite the fact they would have lost to any of the other eight teams, while the Enlighteneds might want to reconsider their team's request to go back to the way things were last year and not bother turning in a lineup at all.

Schmegalicious shakes up a six-pack of whoop-ass and sprays it over Beer 'n Broads 57-52. Supercalifragilisticexpialidoceous must have spent all morning Sunday on their knees - praying, that is - as their town Priest scores 27 (although most priests would rather score with something closer to 10) points thanks to a four touchdown plus 122 yard day on the ground. Lagers and losers get an amazing three touchdowns and 149 yards out of the Buffalo ground game (let's retype that so you realize it ain't a typo - B-U-F-F-A-L-O ground game) and 18 points out of Peyton the Mann yet still leave the table with that post-dinner-at-Wendy's queasy feeling. For the first time in franchise history the Schmegs manage to win their first game under a new moniker (although the stench remains remarkably the same) while the Beers must have slept through their church service with a hangover - how else can you explain scoring the second-most points for the week and still being 0-1?

Da'renegades lay the welcome-back mat for the Horny Toads over a bottomless pit and watch them fall 42-37. The runnin' rebels watch in disgust as the league's second-overall pick can only throw for three points, but two touchdowns apiece out of the Miami and Tennessee ground game - neither of which even existed last year - turn the team's frowns upside down. The amorous amphibians have their return to the league spoiled thanks to shutouts from their first pick and the running back who took the 2001 season off on his own space odyssey. The Gades wonder if their aerial attack is going to be grounded just like last year as %69 of their offense comes from the running game, while the Toads are probably happy just having something sporty to do that isn't centered around kids' sports.

The Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love are the wolves in sheep's clothing that lead Got Sheep? to the 61-38 slaughter. The one-eyed anacondas get nada from either one of their Smiths, but everyone else gets in on the pounding with highlight reel footage provided by their unDaunted quarterback (four touchdowns) and their defense (a fumble and interception returned for touchdowns). The eulogized ewes get three touchdowns and 254 total yards out of their running backs, but both receivers lay the golden goose egg and Air McSquare only contributes one stinkin' TD. The Purples remain undefeated and not in first place in the East while the status monkey hasn't seen sheep pounded like that since the infamous "Getting to Know Ewe" homecoming party back at Iowa State in the early nineties.

The Itchy Tortugas bowl over the Ball Busters 48-33. The Fiji fungi throw a pair of duds out to carry the rock and they deliver the expected nothings, but each receiver comes through with a touchdown and 100+ yards and the quarterback adds three sixes of his own Monday night to put the game out of reach. The cracked bulbs continue to ponder their first two picks at running back, who rack up 354 total yards (+47 from last week) but only three lousy points (-1 from last week), and the quarterback even gets in on this craziness by throwing for 403 yards but only one touchdown. The Torts owner probably wishes his back was more flexible (or at least his neck was longer - much longer) so that they could congratulate themselves on being the only remaining undefeated and untied team in the league, while the Balls have to answer the following multiple choice question:
You know your season is going down the tubes when:
  a) The Edge dulls as he gets stuffed four straight times from first and goal inside the two
  b) The Gannon misfires as he throws a pair of picks inside the two
  c) The Akers aches as he has a field goal taken off the board after the defense jumps offside; two plays later the Torts QB hurls a touchdown
  d) All of the above

The Thundering Curd look mighty impressive for a quivering dairy product as they body slam Da'renegades 46-21. With half of the starters changed from last week the toe cheese try to makeover a preeminently poor team, and it seems to have worked for at least one week as the Hines ketchup man racks up two touchdowns and the defense contributes eight points (actually, digging into the stats reveal the owner, with Spurrier-like precision, benched 28 points in favor of 18 - DOH!). The retreatin' rebels get another fine performance - one touchdown and 194 total yards - from the revamped marine mammals ground game, but that's almost half the team's output as the league's number two pick looks less like Kurt and more like Gus everyday with only one touchdown and an unheard-of sub-300 yard game. The Curd get back to .500 for the first time since Monica was cleaning Bill's pipe under the desk while Da'renegades owner is kicking himself for not making that Warner-for-Fiedler draft day trade.

In a battle reminiscent of Ali-Frazier XIV (yawn) the Horny Toads out-bitchslap Schmegalicious 25-17. The fornicating froggies parade a whole slew of unimpressive performances across the field when all of a sudden, out of the west, some perennial loser named Thomas Jones gallops across Seattle Sound for 173 rushing yards and one touchdown. The schmorgasbord players must have been so horrifically affected by this unnatural feat that they turned tail and fled the gridiron as fast as humanly possible, with only baby Bob Griese hanging around to put up two touchdowns and 119 meager passing yards - even the Priest landed in shutout jail after getting caught with his pants down around his legs. The Hornies taste victory for the first time since the game immediately before that unforgettable 87-33 thrashing they took in ATM Bowl XI while the Schmegs return to reality with the week's low score after registering the high score just one short week ago.

Beer 'n Broads put their drinking durability on display and sneak past the passed-out Enlightened Rogues 28-24. The Busch-buyers and bush-wanters see T-Henry come crashing back to Earth with a big nil and watch with distaste as easy Ed rushes for twice as many yards as caught, but fortunately the Deuce is loose in the Big Easy with two touchdowns and 123 yards. The horrible hobos stink up the joint with the exception of their quarterback and his two touchdowns, then take their frustrations out on the kicker by canning him after his missed FG and two PATs cost them the W. The Broads get back on the winning track but have to hang with the three other teams tied for first place in the West while the Rogues become the only team in the league comfortable with sporting nothing but zeros in the winning percentage column.



Week 3
NFL Schedule
Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love 35 Schmegalicious 38
Ball Busters 20 Thundering Curd* 57
Got Sheep? 43 Itchy Tortugas 36
Beer 'n Broads 32 Da'renegades 51

Schmegalicious break out the head, er hedge trimmers, and lop off the Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love 38-35. Dongalongus gets yet another plethora of single-digit outputs, but the league Priest gets off the boy, er floor, in time to put up three touchdowns and 198 total yards, mostly in the fourth quarter. Penilae purpleous get nothing but single-digit outputs, and have to be especially disappointed when their quarterback skips the game in favor of the Minnesota Booger-A-Thon, even though he wins that contest going away with four monster picks. 'Licious is the model of inconsistency (but what else is new) as they score in the 10's, 30's, and 50's over the first three weeks, while the Loves can't make up their minds whether they'd like to win, lose or draw and thus keep on doing an equal number of each.

The Thundering Curd keep the tubs of margarine rolling downhill as they spread the Ball Busters over the tundra with a 57-20 thumping. The nutty cheese logs get 35 points out of the big green machine as it rolls over the toothless-yet-suckful Lions, highlighted by three touchdowns and 354 passing yards from some guy penciled in on the lineup as "Faver," and add an unlikely 16 points from a dweeb named Lamar who runs through the porous Viking defense. The sack smackers put on the penultimate display in confusion when their number one pick skips the week with a queasy quad and both their quarterbacks have bye weeks, so the mess they send out on the field registers only six sickly points from players not named Akers. For the second week in a row the Thunders send out three different starters and for the second week in a row they register the year's most lopsided victory, while the Busters have but four touchdowns to show from their top four picks in almost four weeks.

Got Sheep? sneak by the Itchy Tortugas 43-36 in the battle of black McQuarterbacks. The mumbling mutton only get two touchdowns from Air McSquare, but an amazing 16 Troy Brown receptions translate into 10 points and Marshall even manages to put six on the board before departing early with a knocked neck. The deep fried bean pies get a sweet four touchdowns from Blab McNabb, but both running backs pitch shutouts and their top two picks can catch only three points. The Sheep? are more than happy to knock the Torts from the realm of the undefeated (but who wouldn't be) while the Itches try desperately to cover up their draft day blunders by making their fourth and fifth transactions of the young season, which puts them on a pace for spending $20 on losses but $50 on transactions.

Da'renegades slip Beer 'n Broads a stiff one (drink, that is) and walk away with a 51-32 drubbing. The raucous rebels bench the worthless Warner just in time to savor four touchdowns and 410 yards out of a man named Brady (here's a story), and 22 points from a pair of 'Phins more than make up for the trio of zeros put up by the rest of the team. The skunky Shiners get 23 points from a pair of Indianapolisians, but that's about all of any value as the team's big Macs - McAllister and McCaffrey - go small and post zilches. The Gades join the gang 'o six sporting 2-1 records although they set themselves apart with the league's only perfect divisional record, while the Beer are bummed to be one of only four teams not in first place.

The Horny Toads hop over the essentially nonexistent Enlightened Rogues 32-5. The perverted pollywogs get a pair o' dozens out of second-year players LaDainian and Vick, but even the lowly six caught by Chicago's Marty is more than enough to put Georgetown's Marty to bed without dinner. The tremendously terrible tribesmen set the tone for the weekend when they start a receiver and defense enjoying their bye weekends, then see things go from bad to worse when their queue-bee leaves the game early with a case of the green-apple splatters - the net result, one FG and two PATs. The Hornies sub-par offense is balanced by the league's best defense (although the Rogues can make any defense look good) while the Enlighteneds are just happy to make it through the week without losing a kicker for a change, especially since he is turning out to be the team's high (and sometimes only) scorer.



Week 4
NFL Schedule

The Horny Toads put the Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love headfirst through the meat grinder 56-24. The worm-eating wartbacks let LaDainian loose for 217 rushing yards and three touchdowns and have Tony go Gonzo over the vaunted Dolphin defense for 140 receiving yards and three touchdowns. The sputtering salami leave both A-Trains stuck in the station but don't really realize they're in trouble until old man Mort kicks half of the team's offense through the goalposts. The Toadies now sport the season's longest winning streak due to the league's stingiest defense while the Love Soldiers need to watch the family jewels as they get locked in the East dungeon with the Ball Busters.

The Ball Busters squeak by the Enlightened Rogues 49-48 in the initial I Got First Pick In The Second Round This Year And Want It Again Next Year Bowl. Both teams suck so equally it is hard to distinguish which side is which. Both teams' kickers hit two FGs and three PATs. Both teams' receivers combine to score two touchdowns. Both teams' quarterbacks throw for four touchdowns. What it all boils down to is a 381-328 edge in passing yards for the busty Balls. The Busters earn their first W of the season but look to be in trouble as the remainder of their schedule is Rogue-free while the Enlighteneds are seeing elevens everywhere as they gain the inside track to that coveted eleventh pick in the 2003 draft while tying the league record with their eleventh consecutive loss dating back to last season.

Schmegalicious whip out the whipping sticks and beat the Thundering Curd into a frosty froth 66-47. After suffering through weeks of underachievement from their number one pick, Kegadelicious is ecstatic to see Shaun the Yawn bore no more thanks to five first half touchdowns and 231 total yards, accounting for an exact half of the team's point total. The mumbling milk duds enjoy a good return on investment when their running backs score two touchdowns apiece without managing to rush for 100 yards combined (heck, the Duce of the north averages a very Bubba Franks-ish one touchdown every 16 yards), but it's not enough to overcome the running back that slew Seattle. The Schmegs display some rare intelligence for benching their klutzy starting quarterback, who can now add dogs to the list of things he can fall over (along with driveways), while the Curds are the most offensive team in the league - but we don't need no stinkin' cumulative point total to tell us that.

Got Sheep? leave hoof prints all over the Beer 'n Broads cans 39-28. The ram-bunctious ones start a broken Brown and get a typical Ram-like performance (zero points) from the league's overall first pick, but their quarterback Airs it out for two touchdowns and 398 yards while their Mouldy receiver rings up one touchdown and 119 yards to make up for it. Suds 'n duds become the first team in league history to start a Texan, and the Carr drives pretty well with two touchdowns but the receivers only net three points and dead-knee Nedney can boot but one PAT. The Sheep take sole possession of first place in the East but probably don't even give a damn since they're still all happied-out following this weekend's butt-whumping of Nebraska while the reigning champs fall a full two games behind after playing a mere four.

Da'renegades scrape by the Itchy Tortugas 18-17 in the season's first pitchers' duel. The dull deserters watch the Giants put dirt Kurt out of his misery after throwing for 17 yards and yet another interception, and the most yards for the week in any category is the 66 rushing out of Ricky, but fortunately for them not a lot of points are needed. The itchies and scratchies catch tons of flack from the fans for starting Randy "Get that traffic cop hood ornament off my car" Moss (although it was by far the best drive the Vikings have had all year), but the doobie in his ashtray must have affected his performance as he catches nada and the rest of the team follows suit. Da'renegades are still one of many first place teams in the West but lose two of their top five picks this week while the Torts are fading fast and need to work on starting the right Minnesota running back for a change - in other words, make their educated decision and then start the other one.

Week 6
NFL Schedule
Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love 31 Itchy Tortugas 30

Schmegalicious* 52 Enlightened Rogues 33

The Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love sneak past the Itchy Tortugas by the pubes on their chinny chin chin 31-30. The pretentious prepuce get three paltry points from their backs and receivers, but Daunte feasts on Lion fillets for three touchdowns and old man Mort hobbles around the field and pooches 10 points through the uprights to save the day. The troubled tortillas actually get three touchdowns from their running game, but their top two picks catch no TDs and their seventh-string quarterback pitches his usual shutout in Tampa Bay to garner the loss. The Purpled Helmets are now just a head's length short of .500 but may run into trouble when Morten is unable to get out of his wheelchair to win a game while the Itchies suffer their fourth straight setback and are forced to live by the old adage "a rolling stoner gathers Moss."

Beer 'n Broads use a single can of beer to get the league's only broad stinkin' drunk and escape with a 41-35 win over the Ball Busters. The horny Hamms get a measly 20 yards combined out of their receiving corps and only one passing touchdown from their number one pick, but T-Henry runs roughshod over Texans for 159 yards and two touchdowns while Vanderjagt offs a career-best 16 points through the goalposts to bail the beer barons out. The onerous orbs answer with a not-much-better 62 yards combined out of their receivers, and only Ahman playing on the New England Green for 157 combined yards and two touchdowns plus a defensive touchdown keep the score as close as it is. B'nB wolfs down some cold ones as a two-game winning streak gets them back to .500 while the Balls only happiness this weekend was the brief 39 seconds between Elam's and Mare's field goals late Sunday night.

Got Sheep? leave the Thundering Curd smelling like rotten goat's cheese after a 44-22 drubbing. The woolen wonders finally get decent performances out of their top two picks, with Faulk marshalling in 158 rushing yards with a receiving touchdown and Chief Green heaving three aerial touchdowns. The curdling custard get three touchdown passes and four PATs from a pair of Packers and - oops, th-th-th-that's all, folks - in suffering as thorough a whooping as some stanky Gator team got down in The Swamp this past weekend. Already with a two-game lead, the Sheep look to be the beasts in the East (although the status monkey hopes they don't try to sleep with themselves now because of this) while the Thunders are still happy despite the outcome because a questionable management move - benching Curtis (129 yards and one touchdown) in favor of Jimmy (49 yards) - didn't cost them the game for a change.

Schmegalicious apply victory block with SPF 30 liberally on the Enlightened Rogues and earn an easy 52-33 triumph. Scrumpdillyicious take the team to Dairy Queen after the game to celebrate their consistent performance, as the quarterback, both running backs, and both receivers find the end zone exactly once each and the kicker chips in five threes and one one to put the game away early. The stupefied scoundrels get only one touchdown out of both running backs and one more out of both receivers, and their doom is official when Drew throws for a season-low 254 yards. 'Licious take over as the most offensive team - although they own that title year in and year out for various other reasons - while the Rogues see their potent one-game winning streak snapped and have to look up the standings at the rest of the league.

Da'renegades thwart the warts with a 45-40 victory over the Horny Toads. As usual, the rebellious runners rely almost exclusively on a ground-based attack as their backs rack up four touchdowns and three performance points while the three players involved in the passing game combine for one lonely touchdown. The procreant pollywogs feel like kicking themselves in the ass - good thing they can't because they're sitting on 'em - after starting the wrong Ram substitute quarterback, but a surprising two touchdowns from the resurgent Raven offense and two more caught Monday night (including an autographed one) take the game down to the stretch anyway. The Gades are tied for first but need to work on developing a more well-balanced offense - which will be tough to do starting either Plummer or Pennington (P-U) this week - while the Hornies should have listened to the babbling Brooks ("put me in coach, I'm ready to play - and score three TDs") and started him instead - after all, he is the team's third pick, right?

The Enlightened Rogues put chastity belts over the end zones to prohibit entrance by the Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love and escape with a 29-22 victory. The hobos from Hades start an old and rarely-used pair of receivers who live up to their billing, but Hearst looks like a Garrison of running backs as he rips off two touchdowns and 130 total yards. The orchid-colored organs get one touchdown out of each running back, but their passing game is nonexistent which again leaves old man Mort as their leading scorer for about the umpteenth consecutive week. In winning two out of their last three games the Enlighteneds see their stranglehold on next year's eleventh pick go down the tubes while the Love Soldiers have their number one pick Cul-poop out for the second consecutive year and have to begin pondering why Iowa doesn't have a professional football team (because then Minnesota would want one).

Schmegalicious outgun the Ball Busters 65-51 in the league's first-ever AM/PM Shootout Bowl. Wanting to put the game away early, Offensivepowerhousecious line up all of their starters 12AM Sunday and race out to a 65-0 lead thanks to two touchdowns apiece from a Griese QB and a Horny WR and three touchdowns and 113 rushing yards from a Priest who finds more desirable holes in the Denver defense than at a convention for altar boys. The nasty nut knockers send out all of their starters starting at 3PM Sunday and manage to rip off 51 unanswered points thanks to three touchdowns apiece from their quarterback and number one pick, but it's just not enough to overcome such a large deficit. While most people thought it impossible, the Schmegs have become an even more offensive team and now stand alone in the West while the Busters find themselves in deep doo-doo with the league's worst defense and a second-round pick - The Edge - who is continuing to look, smell and play more and more like Sue Edge every week.

The Thundering Curd slip Beer 'n Broads rotten cheese dip and leave 'em gagging in the bathroom 46-25. The diabolic dairyheads get a lousy 89 yards out of their Favre-ite quarterback, but the lack of a quarterback makes for a good day for the Green Bay kicker and Fraud Taylor looks like the real deal with 151 rushing yards and a touchdown. The bumbling Budweiser's running backs must have had blindfolds on as they combine for 301 total yards of offense but can only find the end zone once between them, and the game is lost for sure on Monday night when their Indy quarterback and kicker can only manage to outscore some schmuck named Hines Ward by one point. The Curd are smack dab in the middle of things in the East, being two games behind the best and two games ahead of the rest, while B'nB slip into fourth place in the West but can still brag about winning three times as many games as the owner's beloved Vikings.

Got Sheep? string up Da'renegades during a 51-13 lynching. The winning woolen ones are finally appreciating picking the ace out of the bag on draft night as the league's overall first pick goes off for four touchdowns and 235 total yards of offense, which is more than enough to make up for the shutouts served up by two losers playing for Steve Superior and Trent Green-with-quarterback-envy. Ravaged by injury and bye week, the unarmed bandits pin the team's hopes on New York's dangling Chad, and he does provide over two-thirds of the team's offense, but both receivers and both running backs post zeros to put the game out of their misery. Got Sheep? maintain the league's best record and haven't been privy to a beating like this since the cyclonic one that took place in Norman this past weekend while Da'renegades are in search of answers as they are averaging 84 points less than the other teams with winning records.

The Horny Toads break out the Calamine lotion and completely eliminate the Itchy Tortugas 68-15. The sticky-tongued bug-eaters put on a powerful offensive display as every position player scores, with highlights being four touchdowns from last week's benched quarterback and 153 rushing yards and two touchdowns out of LaDainianianian. The scratched rash are totally overmanned as their top two picks catch nothing more than good investment advice and their third pick sits idly by on the bench after earning a bonus bye week thanks to the owner's generosity, and only a two-touchdown Monday night Bus ride keeps this from being one of the most lopsided victories in league history. The Toads must be impressed with themselves knowing that they can leave the NFL's best-ever performance for a tight end sitting on the bench and still win by 53 while the Torts need to stop whining about not getting to play the lineup that they turned in late because they would have lost 68-57 even if they got to play EVERYBODY on their roster.



Week 8
NFL Schedule

Got Sheep? defeat the Purple Helmeted Soldiers of Love 18-14 in the previously unheard-of Only My Quarterback And Kicker Can Score Worthy Points Bowl. The ostracized ovine get one touchdown from their quarterback and less than two handfuls of points from their kicker, and only a 100+ performance from their Mouldy receiver sets them apart in this so-called contest. The sausages-on-a-stick get about the same from their chucker and booter, but their running backs average only 42 yards and their receivers average only 62 yards to really stink up the joint. The Sheep extend this season's longest winning streak to six and can lean on a three game lead in the East while the Purple Soldiers fall into the depths of despair that is the Eastern dungeon.

The Ball Busters defeat the Thundering Curd 35-18 in the previously unheard-of My Two Best Starters "Packed" Up For A Bye Week Bowl. The testis thrashers throw three Davids onto the field and neither receiver from the group scores, but Ahman's substitute brings home the NFL's all-time leading rusher title and, more importantly, nine points to go along with Sue Edge's eight. The shivering Swiss smell great after a running back named Clinton notches two touchdowns and 111 rushing yards, but Ryan's substitute only nails three PATs and Brett's substitute hurls a no-hitter, er no-scorer, Monday night. The Balls eke their way back to within a half-game of .500 but need a rocket ride to reach first while the Thunders barely cling to .500 and are a whopping three games out of first.

The Itchy Tortugas defeat the Enlightened Rogues 34-27 in the previously unheard-of Battle Of Basement Behemoths Bowl. The choking chimichangas have every position player except their kicker register rushing yardage, and even though the two actual running backs average only 8.5 yards between them, their quarterback makes up for it with 111 yards and one touchdown on the ground to go along with a passing TD. The not-so-brilliant bums play hospital hopscotch as a starting back goes down in the third quarter after netting a zero and a starting receiver doesn't even suit up with a screwy scapula, but the game is still close thanks to Corey's 138 rushing yards and one touchdown - and would have been even closer if he hadn't tripped over his pulling guard and fallen at the one-inch line (that's why they call 'em the Bungles, folks). The Itches snap their five-game losing streak thanks to a dose of just what the doctor ordered - Rogues - while the Enlighteneds "win" the battle of cellar-dwellers and now reclaim ownership of the coveted eleventh pick in the 2003 draft thanks to leaving their top pick and his four touchdowns riding the pine.

The Horny Toads defeat Schmegalicious 49-45 in the previously unheard-of Something's Got To Give When The Best Offense And Defense Collide Bowl. The titillating tadpoles start the younger of the two quarterbacking cousins on their roster, and he comes through with two touchdowns on the ground which go along nicely with the two touchdowns and 132 receiving yards from Terrell the Terrible. Varicoseveinus get at least six points out of every position player, but only Yawn Alexander manages to crack the double-digit barrier as the league's best offense puts on a below-average display - for them, anyway - against the league's best defense. And now a few awards to close out the season, all chosen unanimously by a 1-0 vote from the league's panel of expert:

Most Valuable Player: Priest Holmes, Schmegalicious
This year Holmes looked like a man running amongst boys, which is extremely dangerous for a Priest in these times. Coach of the Year: Vince Brunssen, Got Sheep?
Aristotle. Socrates. Newton. Einstein. Even the BCS computer. None of the great minds this world has ever seen can figure out how you win a championship with as crappy a roster as Got Sheep? fielded.
